What it does

One clean path from video to TikTok

No spreadsheets, no copy-paste, no second devices. Three steps, one tab.

Connect securely

Authorize with your own TikTok account through the official Login Kit. We never see your password — only a scoped access token you can revoke anytime.

Pick & preview

Choose a video from your library, preview it inline, and confirm it's the one. MP4 and MOV, ready to go.

Caption & publish

Add your caption and hashtags, then send it straight to your TikTok profile via the Content Posting API.

The flow

How publishing works

The exact path every post takes — the same one shown in our review demo.

1

Log in with TikTok

You authenticate via TikTok's OAuth 2.0 authorization screen and grant access.

2

Select your video

Drag in or browse for the clip you want to post. Preview before continuing.

3

Write the post

Add a caption and hashtags exactly as they'll appear on your profile.

4

Publish

The video is sent to your TikTok account through the Content Posting API.

Transparency

What we ask for, and why

Webdesign CLP requests the minimum TikTok permissions needed to publish on your behalf — nothing more.

Basic account info user.info.basic
To confirm which account you connected and show your TikTok username in the app, so you always know where a post will go.
Publish video video.publish
To upload and post the short-form video you selected directly to your own authenticated TikTok account.
You stay in control
Access is granted through TikTok's official OAuth flow and can be revoked from your TikTok settings at any time. We only connect to TikTok — no other platforms.
