Webdesign CLP Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Webdesign CLP application and describes how Webdesign CLP collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account to Webdesign CLP, we access only the permissions necessary to publish content on your behalf. We request the following TikTok scopes:

  • user.info.basic — your display name and avatar, shown so you can confirm which account you are posting to.
  • video.publish — to post a video directly to your TikTok account (Direct Post).
  • video.upload — to upload a video to your TikTok inbox as a draft, which you then review and publish from within the TikTok app.

We collect your TikTok account identifier (open ID) and the authentication token required to perform these actions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide the content publishing service. We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal data to third parties. Your social media authentication tokens are stored securely and used only for scheduled content publishing.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. Authentication tokens are stored in encrypted form and are never exposed in plain text.

4. Third-Party Services

Our service connects to the TikTok API. Your use of TikTok is subject to TikTok's own privacy policy. We only access the minimum permissions (user.info.basic, video.publish, and video.upload) required to publish or draft content to your authenticated TikTok account.

5. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of all your data at any time by contacting us. Upon request, we will remove all stored authentication tokens and account data within 30 days.

6. Cookies

We use minimal essential cookies for authentication purposes only. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions about Webdesign CLP, contact us at hello@contentloop.de
